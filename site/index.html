What it does

Identify. Explore. Open.

Three things, done thoroughly. AnyFile never renders content itself, so every part opens in the app made for it.

Identifies anything

Built-in signatures and structural probes go first. When they're unsure, it asks Magika and TrID, then falls back to its own analysis of text, encodings, entropy and embedded objects.

Explores inside

Archives, Office files, PDFs, installers, executables, mail, disc images, media and encoded blobs. Expand anything with contents, and keep going as deep as it goes.

Opens in your apps

"Open in viewer" hands each item to the app your system uses for its real type. A spreadsheet named report.dat opens in your spreadsheet app. Save one item or extract the lot.

All the way down

Containers inside containers inside containers.

Files hide files. AnyFile follows every layer, so nothing stays buried.
